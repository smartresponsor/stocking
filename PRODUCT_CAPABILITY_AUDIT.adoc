= Product Capability Audit: Stocking
:revdate: 2026-09-20

== Boundary verdict

Stocking has a modern inventory boundary and now implements the M1 inventory identity/quantity baseline. It owns physical/logical stock facts and reservation/allocation semantics, not catalog products, order lifecycle, shipment execution or location master data.

== Capability matrix

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Capability |Status |Audit
|Stock item identity |IMPLEMENTED |StockItemEntity owns inventory identity and retains Cataloging identity as an opaque reference.
|Per-location levels |IMPLEMENTED |StockLevelEntity scopes quantity facts by opaque Locating reference.
|On-hand/available/reserved/incoming |IMPLEMENTED |Independent quantity facts with non-negative and reserved <= on-hand invariants.
|Available-to-promise |PARTIAL |Deterministic on-hand minus reserved baseline implemented; policy/incoming projection remains M4.
|Reservation/release/expiry |MISSING |Checkout-critical.
|Idempotent reservation commands |MISSING |Required for retries.
|Movement ledger |MISSING |Receipts, allocations, releases, adjustments and transfers.
|Adjustments/reconciliation |MISSING |Operational baseline.
|Transfers |MISSING |Needed for multi-location inventory.
|Backorder/oversell policy |MISSING |Modern configurable capability.
|Incoming/replenishment projection |MISSING |Needed for availability promises.
|Concurrency protection |MISSING |Must prevent oversell under parallel checkout.
|===

== Milestone direction

M1: StockItem + StockLevel + quantity invariants.
M2: reservation lifecycle, idempotency and concurrency.
M3: movement ledger, transfers, adjustments and reconciliation.
M4: ATP/backorder/incoming plus Ordering/Shipping integration.
